2. 수집하는 개인정보

없습니다. 앱은 이름·생년월일·이메일·전화번호·주소·사진·연락처·위치·기기 식별자(IDFA 등)를 비롯한 어떠한 개인정보도 수집·전송하지 않습니다. 회원가입 절차가 없고, 로그인할 계정도 없습니다. 아이가 입력하는 것은 문제의 답과 따라쓰기 획뿐이고, 그마저도 채점하는 순간에만 쓰이고 저장되지 않습니다.

3. 기기 안에 저장되는 것

Full policy (for parents & App Review)

1. Scope

This policy applies to Play with Me! (bundle ID com.wooni.playwithme, the “App”), an iPhone and iPad app made by Wooni (the “Developer”). The App contains number, Hangul, ABC and flag games.

2. Personal data we collect

None. The App does not collect or transmit any personal data — no name, date of birth, email, phone number, address, photos, contacts, location or device identifiers (such as IDFA). There is no sign-up and no account to log in to. The only things a child enters are answers and tracing strokes, and those are used to check the answer and then discarded.

3. What is stored on the device

  • The best star count for each level, and which levels that unlocks
  • The sound-effects and read-aloud switches
  • The arithmetic operations a grown-up enabled (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)

These values are kept in the device's local storage via iOS UserDefaults, are never transmitted anywhere, and are deleted when the App is deleted. The Developer has no access to them. A grown-up can erase everything with Settings → Reset Progress.

4. Sound and read-aloud

So that a child who cannot read yet can play alone, the App can read questions out loud. Read-aloud runs iOS's on-device speech synthesis; nothing that is spoken is sent anywhere. The App does not request microphone access and has no ability to listen to or record audio. For grown-ups who want a more natural voice, the settings screen simply explains where to download one in the iOS Settings app — the App itself downloads nothing.

5. Permissions

The App requests no system permissions at all — no photos, contacts, location, microphone or notifications.

6. Network activity and third parties

The App has no networking code. It contains no advertising SDKs, analytics, crash reporters or social logins, so there is no personal data to share with third parties or transfer across borders. There are no in-app purchases.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for children aged 5 and up and collects no personal data of any kind, including data covered by COPPA (US), the GDPR (EU) and Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. Because nothing is collected, no verifiable parental consent is required. The App has no advertising and no chat, sharing or other social features. The only link out of the App is the link to this policy on the settings screen, and that screen itself sits behind a parental gate.

8. Your rights

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request access to, correct, delete or restrict. The stars and settings held on the device can be removed at any time with Settings → Reset Progress or by deleting the App.

9. Changes to this policy

If anything changes we will update this page and the “last updated” date below. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
