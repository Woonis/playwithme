나랑 놀자! 🔢 Play with Me! 🔢

나랑 놀자!는 다섯 살부터 혼자 할 수 있게 만든 놀이 앱이에요. 아래에서 자주 묻는 것들을 먼저 찾아보시고, 없으면 메일로 알려 주세요.

Play with Me! is a learning game made so a five-year-old can play it alone. Have a look below first — if your question isn't there, just send an email.

⚙️

설정은 어디 있나요?

첫 화면 오른쪽 위 톱니바퀴입니다. 아이가 혼자 바꾸지 못하게 어른 확인이 있어요 — “칠 곱하기 팔”처럼 글자로 적은 곱셈이 나오니, 숫자를 곱해 답을 적으면 열립니다.

⚙️

Where are the settings?

The gear button at the top right of the first screen. A parental gate keeps children out — it shows a multiplication written in words (“seven times eight”), so type the answer as digits to continue.

➕

문제가 너무 어려워요

설정의 연산 고르기에서 더하기·빼기·곱하기·나누기를 켜고 끌 수 있어요. 다섯 살이라면 더하기만 켜 두고 시작해 보세요. 켜 둔 연산만 숫자 놀이에 나옵니다.

➕

The questions are too hard

In settings, Choose operations turns addition, subtraction, multiplication and division on or off. For a five-year-old, start with addition only — just the operations you leave on will show up in the number game.

🔊

목소리가 기계 같아요

iOS 기본 목소리라서 그렇습니다. 설정 앱 > 손쉬운 사용 > 콘텐츠 말하기 > 음성에서 목소리를 한 번 내려받으면 훨씬 자연스러워져요. 앱 설정의 목소리 들어보기로 바로 확인할 수 있습니다.

🔊

The voice sounds robotic

That's the basic iOS voice. Download a better one in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices and read-aloud improves a lot. Use Hear the voice in the app's settings to check.

🔇

소리를 끄고 싶어요

설정의 효과음과 문제 읽어주기를 따로 끌 수 있어요. 읽어주기만 끄고 효과음은 남겨 두는 것도 됩니다.

🔇

I want to turn the sound off

Settings has separate switches for Sound effects and Read questions aloud, so you can silence the reading and keep the effects, or vice versa.

⭐️

단계가 잠겨 있어요 / 처음부터 하고 싶어요

앞 단계에서 별을 받으면 다음 단계가 열립니다. 아이를 바꿔 처음부터 하려면 설정의 기록 지우기를 누르세요 — 별과 열린 단계가 모두 처음으로 돌아갑니다.

⭐️

A level is locked / I want to start over

Earning stars on a level unlocks the next one. To hand the app to another child, tap Reset progress in settings — stars and unlocked levels all go back to the start.

More detail

What's in the app?

  • Numbers, 20 levels — tracing digits, counting, neighbour and in-between numbers, addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
  • Hangul, 20 levels — tracing jamo, building syllables from them, words and pictures
  • ABC, 20 levels — tracing and matching upper- and lower-case, phonics (first sounds), words and pictures
  • Flags, 10 levels — finding flags, from nearby countries to far-away ones

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. On iPad it works in both portrait and landscape.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every game works fully offline, so it is fine on a plane or in the car.

Can a child who cannot read use it?

Yes. Questions are read aloud and answers are shown with pictures and colour, so a child can play alone. A wrong answer only gives a gentle shake — never a red cross — so nobody feels told off.

Are there purchases or ads?

None. There are no in-app purchases, no ads and no subscriptions, and no chat, sharing or other social features.

Do you collect data about my child?

No. There is no account and nothing leaves the device. The privacy policy has the details.

Where do I find the app version?

Near the bottom of the settings screen, next to Version, shown like 1.0 (1). Including it in your email helps us.
